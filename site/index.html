ktv420

Add to Chrome

Desktop Chrome 121+ · macOS, Windows, Linux

  1. Install ktv420 from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Reload Spotify and open a playlist or album.
  3. Click the pink k beside Spotify’s logo to open the overlay. Click the k inside to start capture, or again to stop. Let the songs play.
  4. Once ready, click a song to open its mixer and visualizers on ktv420.
Help

Listening

Captured songs separate automatically. Open a captured track to mix stems, change visualizers, or export MP3s. Seeking or changing songs interrupts capture.

The first separation downloads a 181 MB model, cached for reuse. Keep Spotify open during capture; separation continues in the background. × hides the overlay.

Local audio

Audio stays in this Chrome profile and is never uploaded. Allow about 48 MB per four-minute track for the original and four stems at 320 kbps MP3. Finished audio lives in the website’s local library. Export playlists to keep a backup or move them to another computer.

Updates

Chrome updates the extension automatically. Website updates load when you refresh the page or Spotify overlay.
