ktv420

Listen. Separate. Mix.

Download

Desktop Chrome 121+ · macOS, Windows, Linux

  1. Unzip into a folder you’ll keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions. Enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select that folder.
  3. Reload Spotify, open a playlist or album, then click ktv420 → k to capture. Let the songs play.
Help

Listening

Captured songs separate automatically. Open a captured track to mix stems, change visualizers, or export WAVs. Seeking or changing songs interrupts capture.

The first separation downloads a 181 MB model, cached for reuse. Keep Spotify open while it runs. × hides the overlay; reopen it to resume interrupted work. Opening the player needs internet.

Local audio

Audio stays in this Chrome profile and is never uploaded. Allow about 424 MB per four-minute track for the original and four stems. Uninstalling the extension removes its library; export anything you want to keep.

Updates

Player updates load when you reload Spotify. For an extension update, close ktv420, unzip the new files into the same extension folder, then click Reload at chrome://extensions and reload Spotify. Keep the same installation and profile to retain your library.
