# Disco ball rotation

Reduce the sphere's rotation from roughly 57 seconds per revolution to four minutes at normal playback speed. Slow the moving room reflections proportionally, while retaining their music-reactive brightness and shimmer. This keeps the scene alive without making the ball feel hurried. Retain the existing transport clock so pause and replay remain deterministic.

A twofold slowdown would still feel busy; stopping rotation would lose the mirror-ball effect. Use the fourfold slowdown. Verify existing scene tests, lint, and build; deploy the hosted change. No new tests are needed for this visual timing constant.
