# Restore effect character

The user reports weak/broken-sounding effects, particularly Wah, and explicitly wants center = clean retained. Keep compact controls, bypass, replay/export and effect IDs. Fix the current DSP rather than revert to the old discontinuous routing or extreme resonance gain.

Measured baseline with a deterministic bass/chord/percussion fixture at 44.1 kHz: maximum Wah and Envelope Filter reduce RMS about 16 dB; maximum Echo reduces RMS 10.5 dB and its repeats are attenuated as feedback rises. Vowels lose about 9 dB. Reverb and Saturation are already clearly audible on this fixture; do not indiscriminately increase their gain.

Restore controlled resonant emphasis and body to Wah, preserving exact clean center. Improve Envelope Filter and vowel-band gain. Give Echo a substantial dry path and audible repeats independent of timing. Broaden Chorus/Flanger depth and Phaser stages, with smooth bounded feedback. Keep Telephone, DJ Filter, Tone, Reverb and Saturation character. Protect active processed peaks with a smooth ceiling, while exact neutral/bypass remain sample-transparent. No panning.

Validate character as well as safety: frequency-selective Wah boost with off-band body, center/bypass transparency, echo dry/repeat levels and timing, vowel contrast, envelope dynamics, modulation difference, stereo independence, high-input finite/headroom behavior, automation continuity, tail retention and replay determinism at production sample rates. Measure processor cost and browser worklet/export agreement. No claim that numeric tests replace the user's subjective listening judgment.
