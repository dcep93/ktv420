# Independent DJ tempo and pitch

Approved with yesi. Add two compact controls beside playback: Tempo 0.50–1.50× in 0.01 steps and Pitch −12–+12 semitones in whole steps. Clicking the displayed value resets that control. Tempo preserves pitch; pitch preserves tempo. Apply both to original and stems with a common audio clock. Capture and saved files remain at original speed and pitch. Scene slots are not part of this task.

Use a locally served, pinned time-stretch/pitch-shift implementation running off the UI thread. Keep DSP and playback-clock concerns outside the Player component. Handle processor failures explicitly instead of falling back to pitch-changing playback. Do not repeatedly regenerate or save transformed audio. Preserve seeking, pause, track endings, effects, visualizer timing and aligned stem starts across changes. Default settings should retain untransformed playback quality where the engine permits.

The listening controller maintains an independent remix clock and reports its position/rate to the hosted player and replay. Spotify remains muted at 1×. While prepared stems play, correct more than 0.75 seconds of drift with DOM seeks at most once per second; also correct near the end of slowed songs to avoid premature auto-advance. Preserve the remix clock across these self-issued corrections. Actual remote seeks, pause/resume, and song changes still control playback; advance Spotify when the remix reaches the end. Reset the remix clock before capture, on track changes, and after loss of ownership or the hosted player. Capture remains at 1×.

Record the initial tempo/pitch and subsequent changes in mixer snapshots. Introduce a new recording version for these semantics so old players reject rather than silently misplay it; new players accept v2 recordings with neutral defaults.
